## Accents — three blues, three different jobs (do not conflate)

| Blue | Value | Job | White text on it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upstream accent `tok::accent(0, true)` = `design::accent()` | oklch(0.66, 0.17, 256°) ≈ `#4392F7` | active fills: buttons, selected rows, chips | **3.14:1 — standing user decision D-1, do not "fix" without the user** |
| Brief accent `app_theme::accent()` | `#6C8CFF` dark / `#4666D8` light | ink, markers, dots — carries NO white text | 3.07 / 5.06 (never used as a text fill) |
| Stage dialect | `0x6D8CFF` (`stage_view::SELECTION_ACCENT` and mirrors) | stage selection/gizmo colours, u32 dialect | n/a — stage-side; bridge is DS-12, blocked on D-1 |

Derived glyph variants (RM25): `accent_on_tint` `#93B6F9` dark /
`#3578CC` light and `secondary_accent_on_tint` `#BBAAFF` dark /
`#7A5AE0` light exist only for glyphs on the α 0.16 selected tint
(dopesheet); off-tint glyphs use the plain accents.

## Selected tint and chips

- Selected-row/cell tint: the upstream accent at α 0.16 over the
  surface (`design::selected_fill()`).
- Command palette selected row: the upstream accent at α 0.85;
  its hint chip is the keycap pair — white @ 0.85 fill, black
  @ 0.85 ink (5.12 dark / 5.15 light) — `design::kbd_on_accent`.
- Plain shortcut chip: `control_off` fill, primary-text ink
  (6.32 dark / 13.46 light on the chip over the card) —
  `design::kbd`.
